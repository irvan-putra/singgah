    Temon to Garuda Spark by 09:00: 3 options (unverified estimates, checked 28 Nov 20:15 WIB)

    1. Stay on the Sumber Alam bus to Gamping (stops there 06:50 per Traveloka), then a taxi of about 30 min (about 10 km). At the venue near 07:30, so it makes 09:00. The Sumber Alam 685 listing ends at Temon, so ask Sumber Alam or Traveloka whether you can stay on to Gamping. Not confirmed.
    2. Taxi, Grab or Gojek car straight from Temon, about 44 km, about 1 h 5 min by car (Google Maps driving estimate, 5 Dec 07:30 traffic). Leaving 06:30 gets there about 07:35 to 08:00, so it makes 09:00. Fare not checked.
    3. KA Bandara YIA to Tugu, then Trans Jogja 8 (35 to 40 min, Rp20k regular or Rp40k Xpress per Bisnis.com, Oct 2025; Tugu to venue about 1 h on a Google Maps screenshot). Too tight. The 22 Nov timetable had YIA trains at 06:30 and 08:33, which puts you at Tugu about 09:10. The timetable from 1 Dec (first train 05:17) has no published times yet, so this is unknown.

    Taxi, Grab and Gojek fare estimates (checked 28 Nov 2026)

    Two columns of numbers. Published means a tariff someone published. Own calculation means we multiplied that tariff by the distance. Distances are road estimates (Temon to the venue 44 km and Gamping 9.9 km, from Google Maps driving distances; the Temon point is the area centre, the exact drop point may differ). No live Grab or Gojek app price was checked, because those need the app.

    • Grab car / Gojek car, from Temon (about 44 km): own calculation Rp172k to Rp286k. Published: DI Yogyakarta limit for app car rides is Rp3,900 to Rp6,500 per km (Kepgub DIY 419/KEP/2023, from hargabbmhariini.com, checked 5 Nov 2026). Surge, tolls, airport or long-trip fees can push the app price higher.
    • Grab car / Gojek car, from Gamping (about 10 km): own calculation Rp39k to Rp65k, same published limits.
    • Regular taxi (argo meter), from Temon: own calculation about Rp178k (Rp3,900 per km x 44 km + Rp6,500 flag fall). Published tariff Rp3,900 per km, Rp6,500 flag fall, from harga.web.id (page dated 10 Jul 2025, so possibly out of date). Few meter taxis wait at Temon, so a booked car may cost more: unverified. For scale, published airport taxi prices from YIA (about 45 km) are Rp240k (Taksi Jas) to Rp335k (Rajawali) to Malioboro.
    • Regular taxi, from Gamping: own calculation about Rp45k, same tariff.
    • Gojek/Grab motorbike: published Rp2,000 to Rp2,500 per km in Zone I with a Rp8k to Rp10k minimum (KP 667/2022, hargabbmhariini.com). Own calculation: about Rp20k to Rp25k from Gamping. From Temon it would be about Rp88k to Rp110k, but 44 km on a bike with luggage is not realistic and most drivers may decline: unverified.
